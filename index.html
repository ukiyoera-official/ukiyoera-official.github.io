What would a samurai do on his day off?

I'm a Japanese artist living in a surf town on the coast of Japan. I grew up in Kyoto, surrounded by temples and old woodblock prints, and I spend my mornings in the ocean.

Apparently he goes surfing. He also camps, skateboards badly, and learned the ukulele. The geisha has her own plans: a cocktail at the beach bar, a skateboard, a sunset.

Every design is drawn in the style of Edo-period ukiyo-e, then dropped into modern coast life without explanation. Nobody in the pictures finds this strange. Each piece is printed on demand, so nothing is overproduced.

The samurai is probably still in the water.
